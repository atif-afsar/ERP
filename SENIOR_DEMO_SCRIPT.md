Prepared 6 October 2026. Local fictional training only. No product changes, deployment or new QA audit. All eight accounts passed a real Chromium login check. Passwords are only in Git-ignored qa/artifacts/training/LOCAL_DEMO_CREDENTIALS.md.

## Prepare privately

Keep credentials off-screen and cheat sheet open. Use separate browser contexts for Owner, Teacher, Parent and Accountant. Prepare one NEW unpaid 10000 assignment for a linked sample child, a clearly fictional/non-payable QR/screenshot, unique reference, assigned teacher roster and completed published exam. Use LOG worker. No real money, email or provider checkout. Rehearse once; approvals persist, so prepare a fresh due/reference for the senior.

Requested durations sum to 31 minutes. This schedule trims them to **28 minutes**. Skip optional Library if role switching is slow.

## 1. Opening — 2 minutes

- **Login as:** TENANT_ADMIN.
- **Open:** Dashboard.
- **Click:** Show school name.
- **Say:** “We connect admissions, teaching, collections and accounts in one institution workspace.”
- **Why it matters:** shows responsibility and the module connection.
- **Expect:** School workspace renders.

## 2. Platform concept — 2 minutes

- **Login as:** TENANT_ADMIN.
- **Open:** Users & Access.
- **Click:** Inspect roles without editing.
- **Say:** “Different people get different responsibilities; each institution has separate data.”
- **Why it matters:** shows responsibility and the module connection.
- **Expect:** Current grants visible.

## 3. Super Admin — 3 minutes

- **Login as:** SUPER_ADMIN.
- **Open:** Tenants & Schools → Subscription Plans.
- **Click:** Search school; open Create Plan and Cancel.

- **Why it matters:** shows responsibility and the module connection.
- **Expect:** Tenant/catalog visible; no provider checkout.

## 4. School Owner — 5 minutes

- **Login as:** TENANT_ADMIN.
- **Open:** Master Data → Students → Staff.
- **Click:** Show year/class/section, existing student enrollment/parent and teacher assignment.
- **Say:** “These shared records feed academic and fee work.”
- **Why it matters:** shows responsibility and the module connection.
- **Expect:** Connected records shown.

## 5. Teacher — 3 minutes

- **Login as:** TEACHER.
- **Open:** Timetable → Attendance.
- **Click:** Select prepared assigned roster/date; inspect or save one fictional mark.
- **Say:** “Teaching access follows assignments; roster follows enrollment.”
- **Why it matters:** shows responsibility and the module connection.
